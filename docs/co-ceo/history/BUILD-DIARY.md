\## 2026-10-04 — STEP 0.1



\# Establish Co-CEO Operating Model



\### What Was Built



Created:



```text

docs/co-ceo/OPERATING-MODEL.md

```



The document defines the operating relationship between:



```text

Ball

AI Co-CEO

AI Nexora

```



\### Why It Was Built



Ball wants AI to operate as a strategic partner rather than simply as a task-oriented assistant.



The AI Co-CEO should be able to:



\- analyze

\- challenge

\- recommend

\- identify risks

\- help make decisions



AI Nexora should then execute approved work.



\### How It Was Built



The operating model established:



```text

Ball

= CEO / Final Decision Maker



AI Co-CEO

= Strategic Partner



AI Nexora

= Execution Workforce

```



It also established the decision lifecycle:



```text

Discussion

&#x20;  ↓

Analysis

&#x20;  ↓

Challenge

&#x20;  ↓

Options

&#x20;  ↓

Recommendation

&#x20;  ↓

Ball Decision

&#x20;  ↓

Decision Record

&#x20;  ↓

Execution

&#x20;  ↓

Result

&#x20;  ↓

Review

```



\### Result



The relationship between strategic decision making and execution was formally separated.



\### What Was Learned



A long-term AI operating system needs a clear separation between:



\- human decision making

\- AI strategic support

\- AI execution



\### Next Step



Define the persistent memory architecture.
